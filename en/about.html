About Us

Discover MilesTech — One-Stop Customizable Equipment Supplier

Company Overview

Hebei Miles Technology Co., Ltd has extensive experience in customized automation equipment and supply chain management. We supply four categories to customers worldwide: transformers, packaging machinery, energy-saving screw air compressors, and electromechanical and hardware products, with proven capability in selection and delivery for transformers, customized packaging machinery, industrial air systems and hardware sourcing.

We provide one-stop service from requirement analysis and equipment selection to customized solutions, installation, commissioning and after-sales support. Transformers cover 2 series (dry-type and liquid-immersed) from 30 to 50,000 kVA and up to 145 kV; packaging machinery covers 13 series including granule, powder, liquid and heavy-bag applications; air compressors cover 6 series across two-stage and single-stage compression, VFD and fixed-speed drives, and low and medium-high pressure ranges, allowing plant-wide configuration by pressure and air demand.
