Liquid-Immersed Power Transformer

Liquid-immersed power transformers use insulating liquid for cooling and insulation, delivering efficient heat dissipation, high-voltage withstand, strong overload capability and long service life. The circulating cooling system supports high-capacity, high-load operation, while a robust tank and oil-condition monitoring ensure reliability. Cost-effective and dominant in outdoor substations and renewable energy plants, this is the mainstream choice for medium- and high-voltage applications.

Main products cover 110 kV (IEC) / 145 kV (IEEE), up to 100 MVA, with synthetic ester, natural ester or mineral oil, widely used in renewables, advanced equipment, industrial power systems, infrastructure, generation and transmission & distribution.

Technical Features

  • High-permeability silicon steel and optimised windings significantly reduce no-load and load losses, meeting leading international efficiency standards;
  • Innovative low-noise fans with intelligent temperature control improve heat dissipation while keeping operation quiet;
  • Electromagnetic-mechanical coupled simulation optimises winding support, combined with high-strength insulation for safety under grid fluctuations and fault conditions;
  • Low losses, low noise, low partial discharge and low temperature rise.
