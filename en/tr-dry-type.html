Vacuum-Cast Epoxy Resin Dry-Type Transformer

Coils are cast in epoxy resin under vacuum, eliminating internal voids and substantially reducing partial discharge. The sealed windings offer excellent weather resistance for harsh environments. Low noise, high mechanical strength, strong short-circuit withstand and high overload capability combine to improve operational safety.
